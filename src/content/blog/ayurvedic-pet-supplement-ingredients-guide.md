Consumers today are reading pet supplement labels more carefully than ever. Ingredients like turmeric, Ashwagandha, and Moringa—once associated primarily with human wellness are now appearing in dog supplements, functional treats, and holistic pet care products across global markets.

This shift reflects a broader trend: pet owners increasingly want natural, wellness-focused products that support their pets' health beyond basic nutrition. As a result, Ayurvedic ingredients are becoming a valuable source of innovation for pet brands looking to differentiate themselves in a competitive market.

For importers, distributors, and product developers, understanding which Ayurvedic ingredients are gaining traction can help identify new opportunities, expand product portfolios, and align with evolving consumer demand.

In this guide, we'll explore the most popular Ayurvedic ingredients used in pet supplements, the product categories driving growth, and what businesses should consider when developing or sourcing Ayurvedic pet wellness products.

## Why Ayurvedic Pet Supplements Are Gaining Global Popularity

![Infographic showing increasing demand for natural and Ayurvedic pet wellness products among global consumers.](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/bf38c5_f3cbc6ed38164146a04a6a7268733e41~mv2.jpg)

Pet owners today are becoming more proactive about their animals' long-term health. Rather than focusing solely on treatment, many consumers are seeking products that support overall wellness, mobility, digestion, immunity, skin health, and stress management.

This shift has contributed to growing interest in natural and plant-based ingredients that align with clean-label and holistic wellness trends.

Ayurveda, one of the world's oldest systems of natural wellness, offers a rich portfolio of botanical ingredients that are now being explored across the pet care industry. As awareness grows, pet brands are incorporating Ayurvedic ingredients into a variety of product categories, including:

- Joint support supplements
- Skin and coat wellness products
- Digestive support formulations
- Calming supplements
- Immune support products
- Functional pet treats

For importers and distributors, this trend presents an opportunity to expand product portfolios and enter a rapidly growing segment of the pet wellness market.

## Which Pet Wellness Categories Are Growing Fastest?

![Infographic showing key pet wellness categories including mobility, calming, digestive health, skin and coat care, and immunity support.](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/bf38c5_61cbaac2f23546a4ad6219037e337883~mv2.jpg)

Understanding where demand is growing can help brands identify opportunities for innovation and product development.

### Joint and Mobility Support

Mobility remains one of the most popular categories in pet wellness. As pets age, consumers actively seek products that support an active lifestyle and overall wellbeing.

This has created strong demand for ingredients commonly associated with mobility-focused formulations.

### Calming and Stress Support

Modern pets experience a variety of stressors, including travel, separation anxiety, environmental changes, and unfamiliar surroundings.

As awareness grows, calming supplements have become one of the fastest-growing segments within pet wellness.

### Skin and Coat Wellness

Consumers increasingly associate healthy skin and a shiny coat with overall pet health.

This has led to growing interest in botanical ingredients used in skin and coat support products.

### Digestive Wellness

Digestive health has become a major focus area in both human and pet nutrition.

Products supporting digestive balance continue to gain traction across global markets.

### Immune Support

Preventive wellness is driving demand for products that support everyday health and vitality.

Immune-support formulations have become a core category for many pet supplement brands.

## Top Ayurvedic Ingredients Used in Pet Supplements

![Comparison of major Ayurvedic ingredients used in pet supplement formulations including Ashwagandha, Turmeric, Neem, Tulsi, Moringa, Amla, Brahmi and Boswellia.](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/bf38c5_233acab9d4204890b639c8303db44533~mv2.jpg)

As demand for natural pet wellness products grows, several Ayurvedic ingredients have emerged as popular choices among pet brands and product developers.

### Ashwagandha

Ashwagandha is one of the most recognized herbs in Ayurveda and is increasingly being incorporated into pet wellness formulations.

Pet brands commonly explore Ashwagandha for:

- Calming supplements
- Stress-support products
- General wellness formulations
- Functional pet treats

Its popularity reflects broader consumer interest in holistic approaches to pet wellbeing.

### Turmeric

Turmeric has become one of the most widely recognized botanical ingredients in both human and pet wellness categories.

Many pet brands include turmeric in:

- Joint support products
- Mobility-focused supplements
- Wellness blends
- Functional treats

Its strong consumer recognition often makes it an attractive ingredient for premium product positioning.

### Neem

Neem is frequently used in formulations associated with skin and coat wellness.

It is commonly found in:

- Skin-support products
- Grooming formulations
- Wellness-focused pet care solutions

As consumers become increasingly aware of ingredient transparency, neem continues to attract attention within natural pet care segments.

### Tulsi

Often referred to as Holy Basil, Tulsi has become a popular ingredient in wellness-oriented formulations.

Pet brands commonly explore Tulsi for:

- Immune support products
- General wellness supplements
- Botanical wellness blends

Its strong association with traditional Ayurvedic practices contributes to its growing appeal.

### Moringa

Moringa has gained recognition as a nutrient-rich botanical ingredient and is increasingly being incorporated into pet wellness products.

Applications often include:

- Nutritional supplements
- Wellness powders
- Functional pet treats

Its versatility makes it attractive for brands developing comprehensive wellness formulations.

### Amla

Amla is widely recognized for its role in traditional Ayurvedic wellness systems and is commonly included in antioxidant-focused formulations.

Pet brands often utilize Amla in:

- Wellness supplements
- Nutritional support products
- Functional ingredient blends

Its growing popularity reflects increasing consumer demand for natural wellness solutions.

### Brahmi

Brahmi is another Ayurvedic herb that is gaining attention among product developers exploring innovative pet wellness categories.

Potential applications include:

- Cognitive wellness products
- Botanical supplement blends
- Premium wellness formulations

As the pet wellness market matures, ingredients like Brahmi provide opportunities for product differentiation.

### Boswellia

Boswellia has become increasingly relevant within mobility and joint-support categories.

Pet brands frequently consider Boswellia for:

- Joint-support supplements
- Mobility formulations
- Functional wellness products

Its inclusion in premium wellness products continues to grow across international markets.
