## What Are Yak Chews?

![Close-up of a high-quality Himalayan yak chew](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/34116d_264a30e14ebb47e7810ff2c5e21f3d0d~mv2.webp)

Yak chews, also known as Himalayan dog chews, originate from Nepal and the Himalayan regions of India. They are crafted using a time-honored recipe that blends **yak and cow milk, salt, and lime juice** to create a hard, cheese-like chew. These treats are free from artificial additives, preservatives, and chemicals, making them a **safe and natural alternative** to traditional rawhide chews.

## How to Introduce Yak Chews to Your Dog

If your dog is new to yak chews, follow these simple steps:

1. **Choose the Right Size** – Pick a chew appropriate for your dog’s size and chewing strength.
2. **Supervise the First Chew** – Let your dog explore and chew at their own pace.
3. **Softening the Chew** – If the chew is too hard, soak it in warm water for a few minutes to make it slightly softer.
4. **Reuse the Leftovers** – If a small piece remains, microwave it for 30 seconds to create a crunchy treat.
