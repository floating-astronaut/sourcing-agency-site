### **Why Ayurveda for Kids and Teens?**

In Ayurveda, kids and teens fall under the Kapha stage of life, marked by rapid growth and development. While this phase provides a foundation for lifelong health, imbalances during this time—caused by poor diet, stress, or environmental factors—can lead to long-term challenges such as weak immunity, digestive issues, and difficulty in focusing.

The holistic nature of Ayurveda makes it ideal for young, growing individuals. By integrating diet, herbal supplements, and lifestyle adjustments, Ayurveda strengthens the body and mind while avoiding dependency on synthetic drugs.
