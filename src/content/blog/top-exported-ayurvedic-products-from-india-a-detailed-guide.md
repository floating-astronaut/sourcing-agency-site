## **Understanding Ayurveda and Its Global Influence**

Ayurveda, meaning "the science of life," is a traditional Indian system of medicine that dates back over 5,000 years. It focuses on balancing the body's energies (doshas) - Vata, Pitta, and Kapha - to maintain health and prevent diseases. With a resurgence in natural and holistic health solutions, Ayurvedic products are gaining immense popularity worldwide, leading to increased exports from India.

Consumers today are actively seeking herbal remedies for various health concerns, ranging from stress management and digestive health to skincare and immunity boosting. The demand for Ayurvedic products has skyrocketed, making India a global hub for herbal and holistic wellness solutions.
