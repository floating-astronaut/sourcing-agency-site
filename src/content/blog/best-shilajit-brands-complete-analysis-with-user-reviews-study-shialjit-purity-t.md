### **How to Identify Pure Shilajit**

1. **Origin and Altitude**: The best Shilajit is sourced from the Himalayas and other high-altitude regions. The higher the altitude, the purer the Shilajit.
2. **Color and Consistency**: Pure Shilajit is dark brown or black, with a resinous texture that softens with warmth.
3. **Dissolution Test**: Pure Shilajit dissolves completely in warm water, creating a dark, tea-like color without leaving residues.
4. **Certifications**: Look for brands that perform third-party lab testing and operate in GMP-certified facilities.
