### **What Does FDA Approval Mean for Shilajit Products?**

FDA approval is a stringent certification that ensures products are manufactured under strict quality controls, are free from harmful contaminants, and meet safety and efficacy standards. For Shilajit, an unregulated product in many markets, FDA approval provides:

- **Consumer Trust:** Customers are more likely to trust products with FDA approval, ensuring higher brand credibility.
- **Compliance with Global Standards:** FDA-approved facilities meet international regulatory requirements, opening doors to global markets.
- **Guaranteed Safety:** Prevents contamination with heavy metals, pesticides, or microbial agents.

### **Key Risks of Non-FDA Approved Shilajit**

#### **1. Contaminants**

Non-certified Shilajit often contains harmful substances such as heavy metals, pesticides, or residual solvents, posing significant health risks.

#### **2. Lack of Purity**

Low-quality Shilajit may be adulterated with fillers or synthetic additives, reducing its efficacy.

#### **3. Regulatory Issues**

Importing or selling non-FDA approved Shilajit products can result in fines, recalls, or legal issues, impacting your brand's reputation.
